Homework Section 15.9

  1. Question 1
    Find the Jacobian of the transformation: `x=u^3-v^2`, `y=u^3+v^2`.
  2. Question 2
    Plot the image, `R`, in the xy-plane of the set under the given transformation: `S` is the triangular region with vertices (0, 0), (1, 1), (0, 1); `x=u`, `y=v^2`.
  3. Question 3
    Use the given transformation to evaluate the integral:
    • `int int_R y^2 \ dA`, where `R` is the region bounded by the ellipse `4x^2+9y^2=36`; `x=3u`, `y=2v`.
    • `int int_R xy \ dA`, where `R` is the region in the first quadrant bounded by the lines `y=x`, `y=2x` and the hyperbolas `xy=1`, `xy=2`; `x=v/u`, `y=u`.
    • `int int_R (x^2+xy+y^2) \ dA`, where `R` is the region bounded by the ellipse `x^2+xy+y^2=1`; `x = 1/sqrt(3) u + v` and `y = 1/sqrt(3) u - v`.
  4. Question 4
    Evaluate the integral by making an appropriate change of variables.
    • `int int_R (2x-y)/(x-3y) \ dA` where `R` is the parallelogram enclosed by the lines `2x-y=0`, `2x-y=4`, `x-3y=1`, `x-3y=6`.
    • `int int_R e^((y-x)/(y+x)) \ dA` where `R` is the trapezoidal region with vertices (1, 0), (3, 0), (0, 3), and (0, 1).
