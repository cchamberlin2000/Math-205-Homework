Homework Section 15.2

  1. Question 1
    Evaluate the iterated integral.
    • `int_1^2 int_0^1 (1+4x^3y) dx dy`
    • `int_0^(pi/2) int_0^2 y cos x dy dx`
    • `int_1^6 int_1^2 (x/y + e^x) dy dx`
    • `int_1^2 int_0^1 (2x+y)^(-4) dx dy`
  2. Question 2
    Evaluate the double integral.
    • `int int_R sin(2x+y) dA`, `R={(x,y) | 0 <= x <= pi/2, 0 <= y <= pi/2}`
    • `int int_R (xy^2)/(x^2+1) dA`, `R={(x,y) | 0 <= x <= 3, 0 <= y <= 1}`
    • `int int_R xy e^(xy^2) dA`, `R=[0,2] times [0,3]` (Hint: integrate w.r.t. y first)
  3. Question 3
    Sketch the solid whose volume is given by the integral `int_0^1 int_0^1 (4-x-y) dx dy`.
  4. Question 4
    Find the volume of the solid formed between the paraboloid `z=1-x^2/4-y^2/9` and the square `R=[-1,1] times [-1,1]` that lies in the xy-plane.
  5. Question 5
    Find the volume in the first octant of the solid formed between the cylinder `z=4-y^2` and the plane `x=1`.
