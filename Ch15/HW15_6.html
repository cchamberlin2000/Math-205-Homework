Homework Section 15.6

  1. Question 1
    Evaluate the iterated integrals:
    • `int_(-1)^1 int_0^2 int_0^1 (xz-3y^2) dz dy dx`
    • `int_0^4 int_0^1 int_0^sqrt(1-z^2) z e^x dy dz dx`
  2. Question 2
    Evaluate the triple integrals:
    • `int int int_Q yz sin(x^5) dV`, given `Q = {(x, y, z) | 0 <= x <= 1, 0 <= y <= x, x <= z <= 2x}`
    • `int int int_Q 2xy dV`, given `Q` is the solid tetrahedron with vertices (0,0, 0), (2, 0, 0), (0, 1, 0), and (0, 0, 4).
    • `int int int_Q (2x+y) dV`, given `Q` is bounded by the cylinder `y=x^2` and the planes `y=z`, `x=y`, and `z=0`.
    • `int int int_Q y dV`, given `Q` is bounded by the paraboloid `y=3x^2+3z^2` and the plane `y=3`.
  3. Question 3
    Use a triple integral to find the volume of the given solid:
    • The solid enclosed by the cylinder `x=y^2` and the planes `z=0`, `z=4`, and `x=9`.
    • The solid surrounded by the cylinder `x^2+y^2=4` and the planes `y+z=5` and `z=1`.
  4. Question 4
    Sketch the solid whose volume is given by the iterated integral:
    • `int_0^1 int_0^(1-y) int_0^(3-3z) dx dz dy`
    • `int_0^1 int_0^(1-y) int_0^(1-y^2) dx dz dy`
  5. Question 5
    Consider the iterated integral `int_(-1)^1 int_(x^2)^1 int_0^(2-2y) f(x,y,z) dz dy dx`. Rewrite this integral as an equivalent integral in the five other orders.
