Homework Section 15.8

  1. Question 1
    Sketch the solid whose volume is given by the integral:
    • `int_0^3 int_0^(2pi) int_r^3 r \ dz d theta dr`
    • `int_0^(pi/2) int_0^2 int_0^(4-r^2) r \ dz dr d theta`
    • `int_0^(pi/2) int_0^pi int_0^2 rho^2 sin phi \ d rho d theta d phi`
  2. Question 2
    Use cylindrical coordinates to evaluate the triple integrals:
    • `int int int_Q (x^2y+y^3) dV`, where `Q` is the solid in the first octant that lies beneath the paraboloid `z=9-x^2-y^2`
    • `int int int_Q x dV`, in the first octant where `Q` is enclosed by the planes, `z=0`, `z=y+5` and by the cylinders `x^2+y^2=4` and `x^2+y^2=9`.
    • `int int int_Q y^2 dV`, where `Q` is the solid that lies within the cylinder `x^2+y^2=4`, above the xy-plane, and below the half-cone `z=sqrt(4x^2+4y^2)`.
  3. Question 3
    Use Spherical Coordinates to evaluate the triple integrals:
    • `int int int_Q (x^2+y^2+z^2) dV`, where `Q` is the spherical solid `x^2+y^2+z^2 <= 25`
    • `int int int_Q x dV`, where `Q` lies between the spheres `x^2+y^2+z^2=4` and `x^2+y^2+z^2=9` in the first octant.
    • `int int int_Q z dV`, where `Q` lies between the spheres `rho=1` and `rho=4` and above the cone `phi=pi/6`.
  4. Question 4
    Find the volume of the solid that lies inside the sphere `x^2+y^2+z^2=6`, and below the half-cone `z=sqrt(x^2+y^2)`.
  5. Question 5
    Find the volume of the smaller wedge cut from a sphere of radius `a` by two planes that intersect along a diameter at an angle of `pi/3`.
  6. Question 6
    Evaluate the integral by changing to cylindrical coordinates: `int_0^1 int_0^sqrt(1-y^2) int_(x^2+y^2)^sqrt(x^2+y^2) 2yz \ dz dx dy`
  7. Question 7
    Evaluate the integral by changing to spherical coordinates: `int_(-2)^2 int_(-sqrt(4-x^2))^sqrt(4-x^2) int_0^sqrt(4-x^2-y^2) z(x^2+y^2+z^2) \ dz dy dx`.
  8. Question 8
    • Set up and evaluate a triple integral in cylindrical coordinates that gives a formula for the volume of a sphere of radius `a`.
    • Repeat part (a) using spherical coordinates.
