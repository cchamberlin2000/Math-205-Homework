Homework Section 16.7

  1. Question 1
    Evaluate the surface integral.
    • `int int_S z \ dS`, `S` is the part of the plane `x+y+z=2` that lies in the first octant.
    • `int int_S y^2 z^2 \ dS`, `S` is the part of the cone `f(x,y)=sqrt(x^2+y^2)` that lies between the planes `z=1` and `z=4`.
    • `int int_S x \ dS`, `S` is the part of the paraboloid `x=y^2+z^2` that lies inside the cylinder `y^2+z^2=4` (Hint: use the rectangular form of `dS` with `x=g(y,z)`).
    • `int int_S (x^2+y^2+z^2) \ dS`, `S` is the part of the cylinder `x^2+y^2=4` between the planes `z=0` and `z=3`. (Hint: parameterize the cylinder and use the parametric form of `dS`).
  2. Question 2
    Evaluate the flux integral `int int_S bb(F) * bb(N) \ dS`.
    • `bb(F)(x,y,z)=langle x, y, z rangle`, `S` is the part of the plane `x+y+z=2` that lies in the first octant, and has upward orientation.
    • `bb(F)(x,y,z)=z bb(i) + x bb(j) + bb(k)`, `S` is part of the paraboloid `z=9-x^2-y^2` that lies above the square `0 <= x <= 1`, `0 <= y <= 1`, and has upward orientation.
    • `bb(F)(x,y,z)=x bb(i) + y bb(j) + z bb(k)`, `S` is the part of the cone `z=sqrt(x^2+y^2)` between the planes `z=1` and `z=4` with downward orientation.
    • `bb(F)(x,y,z)=-x bb(i) + z bb(k)`, `S` consists of the paraboloid `z=f(x,y)=x^2+y^2`, `0 <= z <= 1`, and the disk `x^2+y^2 <= 1`, `z=1`. [Note: this requires evaluation of two integrals, and the paraboloid should be oriented downwards]
    • `bb(F)(x,y,z)=y bb(i) - x bb(j) + z bb(k)`, `S` is the part of the sphere `x^2+y^2+z^2=9` in the first octant with orientation toward the origin (Hint: use the parametric form of the flux integral).
