  3. Answer 3
    Both surfaces have the exact same boundary curve `C` (the unit circle `x^2 + y^2 = 1` in the xy-plane, where `z = 0`). By Stokes' Theorem, the surface integral of the curl of a vector field over an oriented surface is equal to the line integral of the vector field along its boundary curve. Since both surface integrals evaluate to the identical line integral `int_C bb(F) * d bb(r)`, they must be equal to each other.
