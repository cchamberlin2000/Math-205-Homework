Homework Section 16.8

  1. Question 1
    Use a line integral to evaluate `int int_S (text(curl) bb(F)) * bb(N) \ dS`, where `bb(F)(x,y,z) = -yz bb(i) + xz bb(j) + xy bb(k)` and `S` is the part of the paraboloid `z = 5 - x^2 - y^2` that lies above the plane `z = 1`, oriented upward.
  2. Question 2
    Use the surface integral from Stokes' Theorem to evaluate `int_C bb(F) * d bb(r)`. In each case `C` is oriented counter-clockwise (as viewed from above) and bounds a surface, `S`.
    • `bb(F)(x,y,z) = (x+z^2) bb(i) + (y+x^2) bb(j) + (z+y^2) bb(k)`, `C` is the triangle with vertices (1, 0, 0), (0, 1, 0) and (0, 0, 2).
    • `bb(F)(x,y,z) = e^y bb(i) + e^(-y) bb(j) + e^z bb(k)`, `C` is the boundary of the plane `x+3y+3z=3` in the first octant.
    • `bb(F)(x,y,z) = y bb(i) - x^2 bb(j) + z^2 bb(k)` and `C` is the curve of intersection of the plane `x+z=1` and the cylinder `x^2+y^2=1`.
  3. Question 3
    Consider two surfaces, `S_1 : z = sqrt(1-x^2-y^2)` and `S_2 : z = 1-x^2-y^2`, bounded below by the xy-plane. Suppose `bb(F)` is a vector field over `RR^3` whose components have continuous partial derivatives. Explain why `int int_{S_1} (text(curl) bb(F)) * bb(N) \ dS = int int_{S_2} (text(curl) bb(F)) * bb(N) \ dS`.
