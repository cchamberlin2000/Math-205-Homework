Homework Section 16.6

  1. Question 1
    Determine whether the points `P(-2,6,5)` and `Q(7,3,15)` lie on the surface given by `bb(r)(u,v) = langle u+3v, 1+4u-v, 5+u+v rangle`.
  2. Question 2
    Find a Cartesian equation for the given parametric surface and identify it.
    • `bb(r)(u,v) = (u-v) bb(i) + (2-v) bb(j) + (4+2u-4v) bb(k)`
    • `bb(r)(s,t) = s bb(i) + t bb(j) + (s^2+t^2) bb(k)`
    • `bb(r)(u,v) = langle u, u sin 3v, u cos 3v rangle`
  3. Question 3
    Find a parametric representation for the surface.
    • The hyperbolic paraboloid `z = x^2 - 2y^2`.
    • The lower half of the ellipsoid `x^2 + 4y^2 + 9z^2 = 1`.
    • The sphere `x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 9`.
    • The cylinder `x^2 + y^2 = 4` from `z = 0` and `z = 3`.
  4. Question 4
    Find an equation of the tangent plane to the given parametric surface at the specified point: `bb(r)(u,v) = u sin v bb(i) + u^2 bb(j) + 2u cos v bb(k)`; `u = 1`, `v = pi`.
  5. Question 5
    Find the area of the surface.
    • The part of the plane `2x + y + z = 6` that lies in the first octant.
    • The part of the hyperbolic paraboloid `f(x,y) = x^2 - y^2` that lies within the cylinder `x^2 + y^2 = 4`.
    • The part of the cone `z = sqrt(x^2 + y^2)` that lies between the planes `z = 1` and `z = 4`.
    • The surface given by `bb(r)(u,v) = uv bb(i) + v^2 bb(j) + 1/2 v^2 bb(k)`, where `0 <= u <= 2`, `0 <= v <= 1`.
