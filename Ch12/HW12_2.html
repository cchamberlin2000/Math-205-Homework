Homework 12.2

  1. Identify and sketch the graph of the quadric surface.
    1. `x^2/4 + y^2/9 + z^2/16 = 1`
    2. `x = y^2 + z^2/4`
    3. `z^2 = 1 + x^2/4 + y^2/9`
    4. `z^2 = x^2/4 + y^2/9`
    5. `z = y^2/16 - x^2/9`
    6. `y^2 + z^2/9 - x^2 = 1`
  2. Use the method of completing the square to identify the quadric surface represented by the given equation:
    1. `y^2 + z^2 + 4x + 4y - 10z + 29 = 0`
    2. `x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - 8x + 4y - 14z = -44`
  3. Draw the space region in the first octant bounded by the plane `z = 2 - y`, the cylinder `x = 4 - y^2`, and the three coordinate planes.
  4. Draw the space region bounded by the surfaces `z = 4 - (x^2 + y^2)` and `z = 0`.
