Homework Section 12.6

  1. Find parametric equations of the line:
    1. Through the point `(2, 0, -5)` and parallel to `bb(v) = 3 bb(i) + 2 bb(j) - bb(k)`.
    2. Through the point `(-1, 3, 2)` and parallel to the line `x = 1 + 2t`, `y = 3t` and `z = 4 - 3t`.
    1. Find a vector equation for the line in 1(a).
    2. Find the symmetric equations of the line from 1(b).
  3. Find the points where the line `x = 1 + 2t`, `y = 3t`, and `z = 4 - 3t` intersects the yz-plane.
  4. Decide if each pair of lines are intersecting, skew, or parallel. If there is an intersection point, find it.
    1. Line 1: `x = 1 + 2t`, `y = 4 + 3t` and `z = 4 - 5t`
      Line 2: `x = 2 - 8s`, `y = -12s` and `z = -11 + 20s`
    2. Line 1: `x = 2 + 2t`, `y = 4 + t` and `z = 4 - 5t`
      Line 2: `x = -8s`, `y = -1 - 3s` and `z = 4 + s`
    3. Line 1: `x = 2 + 2t`, `y = 4 + t` and `z = 11 - t`
      Line 2: `x = -8s`, `y = -1 - 3s` and `z = 7s`
  5. Find an equation of the plane:
    1. passing through `(-4, 1, 3)` and perpendicular to the vector `bb(v) = << 2, -7, 3 >>`.
    2. passing through `(-4, 1, 3)` and perpendicular to the line `x = 1 + 2t`, `y = 3t` and `z = 4 - 3t`.
    3. passing through the points `(-4, 1, 3)`, `(5, -1, -3)`, and `(2, 2, -6)`.
    4. passing through `(-4, 1, 3)` and containing the line `x = 1 + 2t`, `y = 3t` and `z = 4 - 3t`.
  6. Determine the point of intersection of the line `x = 2 + 2t`, `y = 4 + t` and `z = 4 - 5t` and the plane `x + 2y - 3z = 36`.
  7. Find a set of parametric equations of the line of intersection of the planes `x - y + z = 1` and `2x + y + z = 0`.
  8. Decide if the planes below are perpendicular or parallel to the plane `2x - 9y + z = 4`.
    1. `-6x + 27y - 3z = 9`
    2. `-3x - y - 3z = -12`
  9. Find the angle between the planes `x - y + z = 1` and `2x + y + z = 0`. Round your answer to the nearest hundredth of a degree.
  10. Find the distance between the parallel planes `2x - 9y + z = 4` and `2x - 9y + z = 10`.
