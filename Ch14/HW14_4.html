Homework Section 14.4

NOTE: For numbers 2-4, to find a "linearization" or `L(x,y)`, solve the equation of the tangent plane for `z` and replace `z` with `L(x,y)`.
  1. Question 1
    Find an equation of the tangent plane to the surface at the given point:
    • `z=2x^2-y^2+4y`, at `(1,2,6)`
    • `z=2ye^x`, at `(0,1,2)`
  2. Question 2
    Explain why the function is differentiable at the given point. Then find the linearization `L(x,y)` of the function at that point.
    • `f(x,y)=y sqrt(x)` at `(4,1)`
    • `f(x,y)=e^x sin(xy)` at `(0,3)`
  3. Question 3
    Find the linear approximation of the function `f(x,y)=sqrt(20-7x^2-y^2)` at `(1,2)` and use it to estimate `f(1.08, 1.96)`.
  4. Question 4
    Find the linear approximation of the function `f(x,y,z)=sqrt(x^2+y^2+z^2)` at `(1,4,8)` and use it to approximate the number `sqrt(0.98^2+4.02^2+7.99^2)`.

    (Note: the equation of the tangent plane generalizes to any number of variables.)
  5. Question 5
    Find the total differential of the function. For part (b), note that the total differential generalizes to functions of more than two variables.
    • `z=x^2 ln(y^3)`
    • `w=xze^(xy)`
  6. Question 6
