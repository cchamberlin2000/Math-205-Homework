Homework Section 14.5

  1. Question 1
    Use the Chain Rule to find `(dz)/(dt)`:
    • `z=xy^{2}-x^{2}y`, `x=2+t^{3}`, `y=1-t^{2}`
    • `z=y ln(2x+y)`, `x=cos t`, `y=sin t`
  2. Question 2
    Use the Chain Rule to find `(del z)/(del s)` and `(del z)/(del t)`:
    • `z=y/x`, `x=se^{-t}`, `y=1+se^{t}`
    • `z=e^{r}sin theta`, `r=st`, `theta=sqrt(s^{2}+t^{2})`
  3. Question 3
    Use the Chain Rule to find `(del z)/(del v)` for `z=cos x sin y`, `x=u^{2}+v` and `y=u^{2}-v^{2}`. Write your answer as a function of `u` and `v`.
  4. Question 4
    Let `z=x^{3}+xy^{2}`, `x=uv^{2}+w^{3}`, and `y=u+ve^{w}`. Use the Chain Rule to find `(del z)/(del u)`, `(del z)/(del v)` and `(del z)/(del w)` when `u=1`, `v=2`, `w=0`.
  5. Question 5
    Let `z=ln(u^{2}+v^{2}+w^{2})`, `u=2x+y`, `v=x-2y`, and `w=3xy`. Use the Chain Rule to find `(del z)/(del x)` and `(del z)/(del y)` when `x=y=1`.
  6. Question 6
    The radius of a right circular cone is increasing at a rate of 1.6 in/s while its height is decreasing at a rate of 2.4 in/s. At what rate is the volume of the cone changing when the radius is 110 inches and the height is 130 inches.?
  7. Question 7
    According to Ohm's law, `I=V/R` where `I` stands for current (in amps) in an electrical circuit, `V` is voltage, and `R` is resistance (in ohms). As time `t` (in seconds) increases, the voltage decreases while the resistance increases due to rising temperature. Find the rate of change of the current with respect to time when `R =` 100 ohms, `(dR)/(dt)=0.5` ohms/sec, `I=0.1` amp, and `(dV)/(dt)=-0.1` volt/sec.
  8. Question 8
    Use an implicit differentiation formula to find `(del z)/(del x)` and `(del z)/(del y)`:
    • `x^{3}+y^{2}+z^{2}=4xyz`
    • `xyz=sin(x+y+z)`
